BUSINESS POSITIONING CHOSEN
---------------------------
Leenovation Global Ltd is positioned for this website as a design-led PROPERTY RENOVATION,
INTERIOR FIT-OUT & PROPERTY TRANSFORMATION company serving residential, commercial,
hospitality and institutional clients.

Why this niche:
- The company name contains “RENOVATION”, making renovation/property transformation a natural brand anchor.
- The tax certificate describes “Other personal service activities n.e.c.”, which does not establish a construction-specific object; therefore the construction/renovation positioning is a proposed brand concept, not a claim extracted from the certificate.
- The site uses a design-build style because regional firms in this category commonly combine renovation, fit-out, interiors and project delivery.
